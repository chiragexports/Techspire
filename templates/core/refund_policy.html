Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | TECHSPIRE Learning (Indore, Madhya Pradesh)

Overview

At TECHSPIRE Learning (operated as a legal Micro Enterprise / Proprietorship by Vivek Jat in Indore, Madhya Pradesh), we are dedicated to delivering high-quality online technical education, coding tutorials, hands-on skill assessment, and verifiable certifications. We aim for 100% student satisfaction with transparent policies.

1. Course Cancellation Policy

  • Self-Paced Online Courses: Once a student enrolls and accesses digital course materials, lessons, or quizzes, the enrollment is activated immediately.
  • Pre-Enrollment Cancellation: If you enrolled by mistake and have not accessed or completed more than 10% of the course content, you may request cancellation within 48 hours of purchase.
  • Account Deactivation: You may request the deletion or cancellation of your student account at any time by contacting support.

2. Refund Eligibility Guidelines

When Refunds Are Applicable:
  • Double deduction or duplicate payment caused by payment gateway or technical glitch.
  • Technical issues on our platform preventing complete course access that our support team cannot resolve within 7 working days.
  • Written refund requests submitted within 7 calendar days of enrollment, provided less than 15% of the total course video/lesson modules have been viewed and no certificate has been generated.
When Refunds Are NOT Applicable:
  • If the student has accessed more than 15% of the course materials.
  • If a verified course certificate has already been issued or generated for that course.
  • If the refund request is submitted after 7 calendar days from the date of enrollment.
  • Change of mind after completing the course or completing final assessment quizzes.

3. Refund Processing Time

Once an eligible refund request is approved by our academic desk:

1. Review
Review within 24-48 business hours
2. Approval
Direct confirmation sent to your email
3. Credit
Refund credited in 5-7 business days via original payment method
